01 / A CENTRAL QUESTION

What happens when adoption moves faster than understanding?

Organizations are investing in increasingly sophisticated technologies. Yet the capacity to acquire new capabilities does not necessarily produce the institutional capacity to govern them.

My work examines how leadership, strategic alignment, and organizational feedback determine whether artificial intelligence produces lasting enterprise value or simply the appearance of progress.

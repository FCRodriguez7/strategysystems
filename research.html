Research & Inquiry

Investigating how technological capability, institutional design, and strategic intelligence interact in complex organizations.

RESEARCH PERSPECTIVE

Understanding change through relationships.

Technological systems do not operate apart from the institutions that adopt them. Their effects emerge through decision structures, incentives, leadership, feedback, and governance.

My interests center on the conditions that allow organizations to translate new technology into coordinated action and durable strategic capacity. This inquiry draws on systems thinking, digital transformation, organizational leadership, and artificial intelligence.
